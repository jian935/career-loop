# Legal disclaimer

Career-Loop is a decision-support tool, not a career advisor, recruiter, or
hiring authority.

- **Scores are estimates.** A match score is a weighted summary of keyword and
  text evidence. It does not predict whether you will be hired.
- **No guarantee.** Nothing here guarantees an interview, an offer, or any
  particular outcome.
- **Your data, your responsibility.** Profiles, JDs, and reports may contain
  personal information. Review before sharing or committing them.
- **Third-party content.** Job descriptions are provided by you and may be
  inaccurate, outdated, or contain instructions. The project treats them as data.
- **Optional LLM providers.** When enabled, text is sent to the provider you
  configure, subject to their terms and privacy policy. This project is not
  affiliated with any model provider.
- **No legal or immigration advice.** Work-authorization signals in a JD are
  observations, not legal conclusions.

If you use this project at work, check your organization's policies on handling
candidate and job data.
